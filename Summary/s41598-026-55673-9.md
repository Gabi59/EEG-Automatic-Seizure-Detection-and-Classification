# General sumary

### Paper Identification & Metadata
- **Title**: Patient-independent hybrid generative-discriminative modeling for seizure detection in long-term scalp EEG
- **Authors**: Sehar Shahzad Farooq, Abdul Rehman, Jaehyeon Baik, Sejoon Park, and Hosu Lee
- **Affiliations**: Yeungnam University, Jeonju University, and Gyeongsang National University, Republic of Korea
- **Publication Venue**: *Scientific Reports* (Nature Portfolio), Vol. 16, Article 27318, 2026, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-55673-9
- **Paper Type**: `proposal`
- **Task**: `detection` (window-level patient-independent seizure detection under strict LOPO design with temporal postprocessing)

### Population & Clinical Setting
- **Subject Population**: Pediatric cohort (CHB-MIT database).
- **Clinical Environment**: Controlled hospital monitoring environment.

### Dataset & Volume Overview
- **Dataset**: CHB-MIT Scalp EEG Dataset (PhysioNet), sampled at 256 Hz.
- **Subject Count & Internal Inconsistencies**:
  - The authors explicitly clarify that the public CHB-MIT release comprises 24 evaluation cases corresponding to **23 distinct individuals**, because case `chb21` is a later session from the same patient as `chb01`, while `chb24` was added in a later release.
  - Cohort Breakdown: 6 cases are seizure-positive (`chb01`, `chb02`, `chb03`, `chb04`, `chb05`, `chb24`), and 18 cases are seizure-free.
- **Data Volume**:
  - Total evaluated windows: 1,414,710 5-second windows (~982.4 hours).
  - Seizure windows: 876 windows (~0.062% prevalence).
  - Non-seizure windows: 1,413,834 windows.
- **Montage & Channel Harmonisation**:
  - Electrodes vary across patients; no fixed universal 32-channel tensor was forced.
  - A fold-specific canonical channel layout (ranging from 16 to 23 channels across folds) was constructed from the training cases only.
  - Held-out test windows were aligned by channel name, and missing channels were represented via a binary mask. No additional rereferencing was introduced.

# Data sumary

### Data Preprocessing & Segmentation
- **Segment Structure**:
  - Window length: 5 seconds (1280 samples at 256 Hz) with 50% overlap.
  - Labeling rule: Any window intersecting an expert seizure annotation was assigned a seizure label; no seizure duration filtering was applied.
- **Artifact Handling**:
  - `None applied (the study does not employ an artifact-gating or artifact-removal module)`. The paper explicitly states that the pipeline operated directly on prepared EEG tensors without a separate artifact rejection network, addressing noise implicitly through generative training, sanitization, and calibration.
- **Feature Sanitization (Train-Only)**:
  - Missing/Inf values imputed using training medians; extreme values clipped using training quantiles; near-zero variance features eliminated.

### Feature Extraction & Dimensionality Reduction
- **Generative Representation (Fold-Specific Convolutional $\beta$-VAE, $\beta=1.0$)**:
  - Trained strictly on clean interictal windows from training patients only.
  - Encoder: 4 Conv1D layers (32, 64, 128, 256 filters) with BatchNorm, LeakyReLU ($\alpha=0.2$), and Dropout (0.1). Outputs latent mean $\mu \in \mathbb{R}^{64}$ and log-variance $\log \sigma^2 \in \mathbb{R}^{64}$.
  - Decoder: ConvTranspose1D layers reconstructing window $\hat{x}$; scalar reconstruction error $r = \text{MSE}(x, \hat{x})$.
- **Hybrid Feature Construction**:
  - Combines $\mu$ (64), $\log \sigma^2$ (64), and $r$ (1) with handcrafted domain features (log-scaled amplitude, signal variance, relative subband powers across $\delta, \theta, \alpha, \beta, \gamma$, and channel summary statistics). Total initial features $\approx 193$.
- **Supervised Feature Selection**:
  - Train-only LightGBM feature selector reduced the hybrid representation to the top 160 features.

### Classification Methodology
- **Classification Paradigm**: Strict `patient-independent` (Leave-One-Patient-Out / LOPO with 24 outer folds).
- **Candidate Classifiers**:
  - LightGBM, CatBoost, and XGBoost.
  - CatBoost was selected as optimal in 17 folds, LightGBM in 5 folds, and XGBoost in 2 folds.
- **Severe Class Imbalance Strategy**:
  - Retained 100% of seizure windows.
  - Capped non-seizure windows at a 20:1 ratio.
  - Applied positive-class sample weighting capped at 10.0 to prevent artificial 1:1 distortion while avoiding majority class dominance.
- **Validation & Threshold Calibration**:
  - Within each outer fold, training data was split into inner train and validation subsets.
  - Joint grid search over decision threshold $\theta \in [0.05, 0.99]$, temporal smoothing window $w \in \{1, 3, 5\}$, and minimum consecutive-positive run length $m \in \{1, 2\}$.
  - Enforced validation false-alarm caps ($\le 10.0\text{ FAR/h}$ on negative validation patients). Calibrated operating points were applied frozen to the held-out patient.

### Methodological Validity & Leakage Audit
- **Patient-Disjoint Status**: Yes. In every LOPO fold, the held-out patient was strictly withheld from VAE training, channel harmonization, feature sanitization, feature selection, classifier fitting, and threshold calibration.
- **Balancing Timing**: Applied strictly *after* patient partitioning on the inner training pool (safe).
- **Tuning Isolation**: Operating parameters, decision thresholds, and LightGBM feature selection were fitted strictly on inner validation data without access to the test case.

# Conclusions

### Quantitative Metrics & Performance
- **Aggregate Performance (All 24 Held-Out Cases, Window-Level, Table 2)**:
  - Mean Accuracy: **0.9964** (99.64%)
  - Mean Balanced Accuracy: **0.9411** (94.11%)
  - Mean Specificity: **0.9967** (99.67%)
  - Mean False Alarms per Hour (FAR/h): **4.7154 events/hour**
- **Seizure-Positive Subset Performance (6 Held-Out Cases, Table 2 & Table 3)**:
  - Mean Sensitivity: **0.5505** (55.05%) (chb01: 0.8500, chb02: 0.4595, chb03: 0.4110, chb04: 0.5156, chb05: 0.3839, chb24: 0.6827)
  - Mean F1-Score: **0.3543** (chb01: 0.5840, chb24: 0.6860, chb04: 0.0286)
  - Mean ROC-AUC: **0.9496** (range: 0.8850 to 0.9828)
  - Mean Average Precision (AP): **0.2990** (chb01: 0.7585, chb24: 0.4995, chb04: 0.0316)
- **Seizure-Free Subset Performance (18 Cases, Table 3 & Figure 5)**:
  - Specificity ranged from 0.9849 to 1.0000.
  - **7 cases achieved 0.0000 false alarms** (chb12, chb13, chb15, chb16, chb17, chb18, chb19).
  - Median FAR/h was **0.75**, while mean FAR/h was **4.28** (skewed by chb07: 21.80 and chb09: 16.00).
- **Comparison with CHB-MIT Literature (Table 4)**:
  - Contrasted with Peng et al. 2021 (personalized F1 of 88% on 3 channels) and Ali et al. 2024 (event sensitivity 75.34% with Random Forest).

### Key Takeaways & Contributions
- Demonstrates that training a generative $\beta$-VAE solely on interictal data establishes an effective non-seizure reference that maintains 99.67% specificity and near-zero false alarms across many seizure-free subjects.
- Establishes a rigorous leakage-safe benchmark highlighting that all-case accuracy (99.64%) is inflated by non-seizure windows, proving why reporting the seizure-positive subset sensitivity (55.05%) and AP (0.2990) is necessary for honest clinical evaluation.

### Limitations & Gaps
- **(a) Author-Stated Limitations**:
  - Evaluated only on the pediatric CHB-MIT dataset; not tested on adult, ambulatory, or multi-center cohorts.
  - Seizure capture remains uneven across patients (sensitivity ranging from 38.4% to 85.0%), with persistent false alarm clusters in difficult cases (chb04, chb07, chb09).
  - Evaluated at the window level; does not report event-level detection latency or event sensitivity.
  - No standalone artifact-gating module was incorporated.
- **(b) Methodological Limitations [Summarizer's Note]**:
  - Window-Level Metric Skew: Overall accuracy (0.9964) is overwhelmingly dominated by the 1,413,834 non-seizure windows versus 876 seizure windows; balanced accuracy (0.9411) and positive-subset F1 (0.3543) provide the true clinical picture.
  - 20:1 Negative Capping: While necessary to train boosted trees, training on capped subsets discards ~95% of background interictal variability per training patient.
- **Future Work**:
  - Recurrent/temporal attention architectures to capture seizure evolution across windows, multi-center clinical validation, and morphology-aware VAE encoders.
